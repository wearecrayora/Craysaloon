# Data-rights requests

Customers ask from the app (**Your data**). The **salon** is the Data Fiduciary and answers;
Crayora is the processor that carries it out, and the escalation when a salon has not answered in
30 days. Console → **Data-rights requests**, sorted by deadline, overdue in red. No customer names
or numbers appear there, by design.

## A copy of their data (DPDP s.11)

1. *Download their data (JSON)* on the request.
2. Send it to the customer **through the salon** (it answers for it), by a channel the customer
   used - never to an address you found yourself.
3. *Mark as sent*, saying how. The customer reads that sentence under **Your data**.

## Erasure (s.12)

*Carry out erasure* on the request, with a reason. Name, number, birthday and login link go;
bookings, payments and the wallet ledger stay against an anonymous record, as tax law requires.
The customer's binding is released. The outcome is written for them automatically.

## A complaint (grievance)

The salon's privacy contact answers (their Privacy contact page in the console). If it is overdue,
call them first; escalate internally if they do not act.

## Never

- Let a request pass its due date silently. Overdue is a breach of the Act, not a backlog.
- Answer a request by customer name or phone lookup - only through the request.
