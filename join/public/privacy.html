Privacy at Cray Salon

Last updated 28 September 2026. This page covers the Cray Salon app and the service behind it.

Cray Salon is used by one salon at a time. When you scan a salon’s QR code and log in, you are joining that salon — and your details, your visits and your wallet belong to it alone. No other salon on Cray Salon can see that you exist.

The short version

  • Your salon decides what it collects about you and why. It is who you ask about your data.
  • Crayora builds and runs the software for your salon. We handle your data only to make the app work, on your salon’s instructions.
  • We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with other salons, ever.
  • Marketing messages are off unless you turn them on, and you can turn them off at any time in Your data in the app.
  • You can ask for a copy of your data, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted. Money records stay, but with your name removed.

Who holds your data

Your salon is the Data Fiduciary. Under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the Data Fiduciary is whoever decides why and how your personal data is handled. That is the salon you joined: it decides to keep your visit history, to remind you when a haircut is due, and to run its own loyalty scheme. Its name and its privacy contact are shown to you in the app before you log in — on the screen where you are asked to agree.

We do not decide what your salon collects, we do not use your data for our own purposes, and we do not build any profile of you across salons.

In practice that means: ask your salon first. If your salon does not answer within 30 days, or you are not satisfied, escalate to us — the addresses are at the bottom of this page.

A note on your mobile number

Your number is stored twice, deliberately: once as the number itself, so the salon can call you, and once as a salted one-way fingerprint that cannot be turned back into a number. The fingerprint is what enforces the rule that one number belongs to one salon. It is also what survives if you ask to be erased — see below.

What we never do

  • We never sell your data, and we never rent, trade or share it for anyone else’s advertising.
  • No salon can see another salon’s customers. This is enforced by the database itself, not by our good intentions, and it is tested automatically on every single change we make. A test that deliberately creates a leak has to fail the build, or the build does not ship.
  • Other customers of your salon cannot see you. Same mechanism: your rows are yours.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites. There is no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK and no social login in the app. This page has none either.
  • We do not read your salon’s payment or messaging credentials back. There is no screen and no interface anywhere that can show one — not to the salon, and not to us.

The messages you get

There are two kinds, and they are treated differently.

Service messages tell you about something you did: a booking confirmed, a visit completed, money added to your wallet, a reminder that your next appointment is due. These are the service itself. They cannot be switched off while your account exists — if you want them to stop, ask for the account to be deleted. We keep them to what is necessary.

Promotional messages are offers and campaigns. These need your consent, they are off until you give it, and withdrawing it is one tap in Your data in the app. Withdrawal takes effect immediately. WhatsApp and photo consents are asked for separately, and agreeing to one is never agreeing to another.

Where possible your salon reaches you through an app notification rather than SMS or WhatsApp, which is cheaper for the salon and quieter for you.

Your rights, and how to use them

The DPDP Act gives you these. In the app they live on one screen: Your data.

  • Know what is held about you and who it has been shared with — ask for a copy (s.11).
  • Correct or complete it if it is wrong (s.12). You can edit your own profile in the app, or ask the salon.
  • Have it erased (s.12(3)) — see the next section for exactly what that means.
  • Withdraw consent as easily as you gave it (s.6(4)). The toggles are in the app and take effect at once.
  • Complain to your salon’s named privacy contact, and get an answer (s.13).
  • Nominate someone to exercise these rights for you if you die or become incapable (s.14). Ask your salon’s privacy contact.

Requests are answered within 30 days. If a request has to be refused or limited — for example, financial records the law requires the salon to keep — you are told that, and told why. A silent refusal is not an answer.

What erasure actually does

We do not pretend to delete things we cannot delete. When your erasure request is carried out:

  • Your name, mobile number and date of birth are removed, and the link between your account and your login is cut. Your notification tokens are deleted.
  • Your financial records stay — top-ups, spends, refunds — but they now point at someone nobody can name. These are the salon’s books; tax law requires them to be kept, and we are not allowed to delete them.
  • The one-way fingerprint of your number stays. It is not readable as a number, and it is the only thing that keeps “one number, one salon” true after you are gone. Without it, an erased account could quietly become two.

Children

Cray Salon is for adults booking their own appointments. A salon must not create an account for anyone under 18 without verifiable consent from a parent or guardian, and must not use a child’s data for tracking or targeted advertising — the DPDP Act forbids both. If you believe a child’s details are held here, write to the addresses below and they will be removed.

How your data is protected

  • Separation is enforced by the database, not by application code that could forget. Every table carries the salon it belongs to, and the rules are switched on and forced — no query, however written, can return another salon’s rows.
  • Provider credentials are write-only. A salon’s payment and messaging keys can be set and replaced, never read back, by anyone.
  • Money moves through one path. Wallet and loyalty records can only be added to, never edited or deleted; a correction is a new line that says what it corrects. No salon owner, manager or staff member has any way to adjust a balance by hand.
  • Logging in requires a code sent to your number. Console access for Crayora staff requires a second factor, and every administrative action is recorded against a named person.
  • We test for leaks on purpose. Our build deliberately creates a broken table, an unprotected function and an open payment path, and fails if the safety checks do not catch them. A check that has only ever been seen passing is not known to work.

If something goes wrong

If personal data here is exposed, lost or accessed without authority, we tell your salon immediately, we report to CERT-In within 6 hours as the directions require, the Data Protection Board is informed without delay and in full within 72 hours, and you are told directly — what happened, what it means for you, what has been done, and what you should do. You will not learn about it from somewhere else.
