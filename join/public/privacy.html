Privacy at Cray Salon

Effective 12 October 2026. This page covers the Cray Salon app and the service behind it. यह सूचना ऐप में हिन्दी में भी उपलब्ध है।

Cray Salon is used by one salon at a time. When you scan a salon’s QR code and log in, you are joining that salon — and your details, your visits and your wallet belong to it alone. No other salon on Cray Salon can see that you exist.

The short version

  • Your salon decides what it collects about you and why. It is who you ask about your data.
  • Crayora builds and runs the software for your salon. We handle your data only to make the app work, on your salon’s instructions.
  • We do not sell your data, we do not use it for advertising, and we never share it with other salons.
  • Offers and “you are due for a visit” reminders are off unless you switch them on, and one tap in Your data in the app switches them off.
  • You can get a copy of your data, correct it, or have it deleted. Money records stay, with your name removed, because tax law requires it.

Who holds your data

Your salon is the Data Fiduciary. Under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the Data Fiduciary is whoever decides why and how your personal data is handled. That is the salon you joined: it decides to keep your visit history, to remind you when a service is due, and to run its own wallet and referral offers. Its name and its privacy contact — a named person, with an email or a phone number — are shown to you in the app before you log in, on the screen where you are asked to agree.

Crayora is the Data Processor. Crayora makes Cray Salon. We store and process your data on behalf of your salon and under our agreement with it. We do not decide what your salon collects, we do not use your data for our own purposes, and we do not build any profile of you across salons.

In practice: ask your salon first. If it does not answer within 30 days, or you are not satisfied, write to us — see Who to contact.

A note on your mobile number

Your number is stored twice, deliberately: once as the number itself, so the salon can call you, and once as a keyed one-way fingerprint that cannot be turned back into a number. The fingerprint is what enforces the rule that one number belongs to one salon. It is also what survives if you ask to be erased — see below.

We never ask for your contacts, your location, your photo gallery, or access to anything else on your phone. The camera is used only if you choose to scan a salon’s QR code, and nothing it sees is kept.

On what basis

  • To run the service you signed up for — your account, bookings, visits, wallet, payments, receipts and service messages. You gave these details to your salon for exactly this, which the Act allows without a separate consent (s.7(a)).
  • Your consent — offers and due-for-a-visit reminders, WhatsApp, and photos. Each is asked for separately, and each can be withdrawn as easily as it was given (s.6(4)). Withdrawing one never affects the others, or the service itself.
  • The law — keeping financial records for as long as tax law requires, and answering lawful requests from authorities.

Nothing about you is decided automatically: there is no scoring, profiling or automated decision that affects what you are offered or charged.

What we never do

  • We never sell your data, and we never rent, trade or share it for anyone else’s advertising.
  • No salon can see another salon’s customers. This is enforced by the database itself, not by our good intentions, and it is tested automatically on every change we make. A test that deliberately creates a leak has to fail the build, or the build does not ship.
  • Other customers of your salon cannot see you, and you cannot see them. Same mechanism: your rows are yours.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites. There is no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK and no social login in the app. This page has none either, and sets no cookies.
  • Nobody reads your salon’s payment or messaging credentials back. There is no screen and no interface anywhere that can show one — not to the salon, and not to us.

The messages you get

Service messages are about something you did: your login code, a booking confirmed or changed, a bill ready to pay, money added to your wallet. They are part of the service and come while your account exists. If you want them to stop, ask for the account to be deleted.

Promotional messages are offers, campaigns, and reminders that you are due for your next visit. These need your consent, they are off until you give it, and withdrawing it is one tap in Your data, taking effect at once. WhatsApp is asked for separately: agreeing to offers is never agreeing to WhatsApp.

Your salon reaches you through an app notification first. Only if that does not arrive does it fall back to WhatsApp (if you agreed to it) or SMS — which the salon pays for, so it does not do it lightly.

Your rights, and how to use them

The DPDP Act gives you these. In the app they are on one screen: Your data (from the shield on the home screen, or Me → Your data).

  • Know what is held about you and who it has been shared with — ask for a copy (s.11).
  • Correct or complete it if it is wrong (s.12). Tell your salon’s privacy contact — shown under Your data — and it is corrected.
  • Have it erased (s.12(3)) — see exactly what that means.
  • Withdraw consent as easily as you gave it (s.6(4)). The switches are in the app and take effect at once.
  • Complain to your salon’s named privacy contact, and get an answer (s.13).
  • Nominate someone to exercise these rights for you if you die or become unable to (s.14). Ask your salon’s privacy contact.

Every request is answered within 30 days, and the app shows you the date it is due. If a request has to be refused or limited — for example, financial records the law requires the salon to keep — you are told so, and told why. A silent refusal is not an answer.

Deleting your account

In the app: open Your data and choose to have your data deleted. The screen tells you what will be removed and what must be kept before you confirm.

Without the app: write to your salon’s privacy contact, or to us at the address under Who to contact, from the number’s owner, naming the salon. We will confirm it is you before acting.

We do not pretend to delete things we cannot delete. When an erasure is carried out:

  • Your name, mobile number, birthday and anniversary are removed, and the link between your account and your login is cut. Your notification tokens are deleted. You can no longer log in to that salon.
  • Your financial records stay — top-ups, spends, receipts and invoices — but they now point at someone nobody can name. These are the salon’s books; tax law requires them to be kept, and they may not be deleted.
  • The one-way fingerprint of your number stays. It is not readable as a number, and it is the only thing that keeps “one number, one salon” true after you are gone.
  • Wallet credit cannot be withdrawn as cash and is lost with the account. Spend it, or ask the salon about it, before you ask for deletion.

Children

Cray Salon is for adults booking their own appointments. A salon must not create an account for anyone under 18 without verifiable consent from a parent or guardian, and no one may use a child’s data for tracking, behavioural monitoring or targeted advertising — the DPDP Act forbids it. If you believe a child’s details are held here, write to us and they will be removed.

How your data is protected

  • Separation is enforced by the database, not by application code that could forget. Every table carries the salon it belongs to, and the rules are switched on and forced — no query, however written, can return another salon’s rows.
  • Everything travels encrypted between your phone and our servers, and is encrypted at rest by our hosting providers.
  • Provider credentials are write-only. A salon’s payment and messaging keys can be set and replaced, never read back, by anyone.
  • Money moves through one path. Wallet records, receipts and invoices can only be added to, never edited or deleted. No salon owner, manager or staff member has any way to adjust a balance by hand.
  • Logging in requires a code sent to your number. Crayora’s console sits behind an access gate and its own two-factor login, and every administrative action is recorded against a named person. Looking at a salon’s customers for support needs a stated reason and ends automatically.
  • We test for leaks on purpose. Our build deliberately creates a broken table, an unprotected function and an open payment path, and fails if the safety checks do not catch them.

If something goes wrong

If personal data here is exposed, lost or accessed without authority, we tell your salon immediately, we report to CERT-In within 6 hours, the Data Protection Board is informed without delay and in full within 72 hours, and you are told directly — what happened, what it means for you, what has been done, and what you should do. You will not learn about it from somewhere else.
