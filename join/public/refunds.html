Refunds and cancellations

Effective 14 October 2026.

You pay for a salon visit after it, so a booking you cancel never needs a refund. What needs explaining is the wallet, and the rare payment that goes wrong.

The short version

  • Cancelling a booking costs nothing in the app; your salon’s cancellation policy, if it has one, is shown when you book.
  • Wallet top-ups are not refundable — but the credit never expires, so it is never lost.
  • A failed, duplicate or wrong payment is put right, to the way you paid.

Cancelling a booking

Cancel an upcoming booking in the app, where it offers to, or tell the salon. Nothing has been paid yet, so there is nothing to refund. If your salon has a cancellation policy — for example, a late cancellation it asks you to call about — it is shown in the app.

A service you were not happy with

Talk to your salon. Whether a service is redone, discounted or refunded is the salon’s decision, and the salon pays any refund, because it was paid to the salon.

Wallet top-ups

Money you add to your wallet is prepaid credit with your salon. It is not refundable and cannot be withdrawn as cash. It is also never lost: credit you paid for never expires, and you can spend it on any of the salon’s services. The app shows you this before you pay.

Bonus credit and referral rewards are a gift from the salon: they have no cash value and are never refunded.

When money comes back

  • A payment failed but money left your account. Your bank or Razorpay reverses it automatically, to the way you paid, usually within 5–7 working days. If it has not arrived after that, write to us with the UPI or transaction reference.
  • You were charged twice, or the wrong amount. Tell your salon or write to us with the reference. The extra is refunded by your salon, through Razorpay, to the way you paid.
  • Your salon stops using Cray Salon, or closes. Your salon settles your unspent paid credit with you before it leaves.

How to ask

Ask at your salon’s counter, or use the salon contact in the app. If your salon does not answer, write to Crayora at crayoratech@gmail.com, naming your salon and the payment. You will get an answer within 30 days. Crayora never holds your money, so a refund is always paid by your salon or reversed by Razorpay — we make sure it happens.
