Cookies

Effective 14 October 2026.

This website sets no cookies. It has no analytics, no advertising and no trackers, and it stores nothing on your device. That is why there is no cookie banner here: there is nothing to agree to.

The app

The Cray Salon app does not use cookies either. To work, it keeps a few things on your phone:

WhatWhy
Your sign-inSo you stay signed in to your salon.
Your salon’s look — name, logo, coloursSo the app looks like your salon even when you are offline.
Your languageSo the app stays in the language you chose.
A random device codeTo stop someone flooding a salon with join attempts. It identifies the phone, not you, and is never used for advertising.
Today’s bookings (salon staff only)So the salon can mark visits complete when the internet drops.

None of it is used to track you or to show you advertising, and all of it is removed when you uninstall the app. Android does not back it up to your Google account.

The app downloads its typefaces from Google Fonts, so Google sees your phone’s internet address when it does. When the app crashes, a technical report goes to Sentry so the fault can be fixed. Both are listed, with everyone else who handles data for the app, in the privacy policy.

The Crayora console

Crayora’s own staff sign in to a separate console to set salons up. It uses only the cookies that keep them signed in. Salon customers never see it.

If this changes

If we ever add a cookie that is not strictly needed, this page will say so first, and you will be asked before it is set.

Cray Salon is made by Crayora, India. This page carries no trackers, cookies or analytics.
