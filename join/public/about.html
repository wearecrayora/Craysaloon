About Crayora

Business details, as of 14 October 2026.

Crayora makes Cray Salon: a salon’s own app for bookings, bills, a prepaid wallet, reminders and refer-a-friend — set up for each salon, in its name and colours.

Who does what

Each salon is an independent business. It sets its services and prices, provides them, takes your payments into its own account, and decides what it keeps about you — for your data it is the “Data Fiduciary”.

Crayora builds the app and runs the service behind it for those salons, and handles your data only to make the app work, on the salon’s instructions. Crayora never holds customers’ money.

For salons

Cray Salon is set up for each salon by Crayora — there is no sign-up. To ask about it, write to crayoratech@gmail.com.
