Cray Salon

Delete your account and data

You can ask for your Cray Salon account to be deleted at any time. Every request is answered within 30 days.

How to ask

In the app

Open Me → Your data and choose to have your data deleted. The screen shows what will be removed and what must be kept before you confirm.

Without the app

Write to your salon’s privacy contact (shown in the app and in the salon), or to crayoratech@gmail.com, from the number the account uses, naming the salon. We confirm it is you before acting.

What is deleted

  • Your name, mobile number, birthday and anniversary, and the link between your account and your login. Your notification tokens are deleted. You can no longer sign in to that salon.

What is kept, and why

  • Your financial records - top-ups, spends, receipts and invoices - stay, but point at someone nobody can name. They are the salon’s books, and tax law requires them to be kept.
  • A one-way fingerprint of your number stays. It cannot be read back as a number; it only keeps “one number, one salon” true after you are gone.
  • Wallet credit cannot be withdrawn as cash and is lost with the account. Spend it, or ask the salon about it, before you ask for deletion.

The full detail is in the privacy policy.
